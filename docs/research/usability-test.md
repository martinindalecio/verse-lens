# Usability test: person cards (5 people, 15 minutes each)

Goal: find out whether people discover the tappable names, understand the card, and can find their way back to where they were reading. Test the clickable mockup now; repeat with the real prototype later.

## Who

Five people, mixed: at least two who read the Bible regularly, at least one who rarely does, at least one aged 60+, and if possible one who uses large text on their phone. Friends and church members are fine. Do not explain the app beforehand.

## Before you start (2 min)

- Ask permission: "Can I watch you try this and take notes? It's the app being tested, not you. There are no wrong answers. Can I record your screen?" Only record if they say yes, and delete recordings once you've written your notes.
- Hand them the phone with the mockup open on Acts 9.
- Ask them to think out loud: "Say what you're looking at and what you expect to happen."

## Tasks (10 min)

Read each task aloud. Don't help unless they're stuck for more than a minute; note where they got stuck.

1. **Discovery.** "Read the first few verses as you normally would." Then: "What can you do on this screen?"
   *Watch:* do they notice the underlined names without being told? Do they tap a name or a verse?
2. **Who is this?** "Find out who the Saul in verse 11 is."
   *Watch:* how long it takes; whether they read the "So far" timeline; whether they mention Paul or King Saul.
3. **Follow the thread.** "Find out who Stephen was, and then who else was chosen with him."
   *Watch:* do they use the "Connected to" chips? Do they get lost?
4. **Find your way back.** "Now go back to where you were reading in Acts 9."
   *Watch:* can they do it without help? How many taps? Do they look for a back button?
5. **What's happening here?** "Find out what's going on in verse 10."
   *Watch:* do they find the verse tap / Passage tab?

## Quick questions (3 min)

1. Show Saul's card for 5 seconds, then hide it. "Who is he? Is he the same as King Saul?"
2. "What do you think 'AI' means on the card? Does it make you trust the summaries more or less?"
3. "Was anything confusing or annoying?"
4. "Would you use this when you read the Bible? When?"
5. Older testers or large-text users: "Was the text comfortable to read? Did you want the panel bigger?"

## What to record (per person)

| | Result |
|---|---|
| Found tappable names unaided? | yes / no / after hint |
| Task 2 time | seconds |
| Got back to Acts 9 unaided? | yes / no, taps: |
| Mis-taps (name vs. verse) | count |
| Said Saul ≠ King Saul? | yes / no |
| Meaning of "AI" | their words |
| Biggest confusion | their words |

## Afterwards

Look for problems that 2 or more of the 5 hit; those get fixed first. Add findings to `docs/research/` and update the design doc.
